## Option A: Short Evidence-Based Conclusion

*Structure*: A concise, direct summary focusing strictly on the primary numerical observations and key empirical takeaways.

### Draft Outline:
1. **Restatement of Research Question**:
   - Briefly state that this independent study investigated capital allocation across eight major investment-banking firms from 2020 through 2024.
2. **Summary of Primary Quantitative Evidence**:
   - The eight firms returned $233.2 billion to shareholders ($113.8B in cash dividends and $119.3B in share repurchases).
   - Cash dividends exhibited continuous baseline stability (+92.5% at Goldman Sachs, +124.1% at Morgan Stanley), whereas share repurchases varied widely across peak and trough years.
   - Bank holding companies maintained Standardized CET1 ratios between 13.1% and 17.4%, while advisory boutique compensation ratios rose to 66%–71% during lower-revenue periods as operating margins compressed.
   - In the small exploratory lagged analysis ($t 	o t+1$), prior-year share repurchases showed a near-zero linear relationship with next-year ROE in Panel A ($r = -0.049$, $N=16$).
3. **Factual Closing Statement**:
   - Reiterate that while capital allocation structures differed significantly across bank holding companies and advisory boutiques, both models operated within the broader macroeconomic cycle.

## Option B: Question-by-Question Structured Conclusion

*Structure*: An organized conclusion that addresses each component of the core research question in sequential order.

### Draft Outline:
1. **How Did Major Firms Allocate Capital?**:
   - Discuss the division between business investment (personnel compensation and technology expenses), regulatory capital reserves (CET1 and Tier 1 leverage buffers in BHCs; cash cushions in boutiques), and shareholder distributions (cash dividends and common share repurchases).
2. **How Did Capital Allocation Change Over Time?**:
   - Describe the five-year trajectory: conservative distributions during the 2020 pandemic onset, record share repurchases during the 2021 fee boom, curtailment of buybacks and compensation ratio expansion during the 2022–2023 downturn, and recovery in 2024.
3. **Were Capital Allocation Differences Associated with Subsequent Performance?**:
   - Report the exploratory lagged findings: in this small five-year dataset, one-year lagged capital allocation variables exhibited weak or inconsistent associations with next-year operating profitability (ROE in BHCs, operating margin in boutiques).
4. **Concluding Synthesis**:
   - Summarize that the data illustrates distinct capital management frameworks across regulated banks and asset-light boutiques, but does not show a uniform link between prior-year payouts and subsequent profitability.

## Option C: Synthesis of Main Finding, Critical Limitations, and Future Research

*Structure*: A comprehensive conclusion that highlights the main empirical pattern, balances it with explicit study limitations, and outlines future research pathways.

### Draft Outline:
1. **Core Empirical Pattern**:
   - Highlight the fundamental structural contrast: bank holding companies managed capital within balance-sheet and regulatory solvency constraints, while advisory boutiques operated asset-light models where personnel compensation ratios absorbed revenue swings.
2. **Primary Limitations of the Study**:
   - Explicitly disclose that the study analyzed an observational dataset covering eight firms over five fiscal years (yielding small lagged samples of $N=12$ to $N=16$).
   - Note that the 2020–2024 period included historic macroeconomic shocks (pandemic monetary stimulus, zero interest rates, followed by rapid 525-basis-point rate hikes), making it impossible to isolate pure capital allocation effects from macro factors.
   - Emphasize that correlation cannot establish causality or executive intent.
3. **Directions for Future Research**:
   - Suggest extending the sample across multiple economic cycles (such as 2005–2025) to capture both the 2008 Global Financial Crisis and recent market shifts.
   - Propose examining quarterly financial disclosures to capture intra-year capital allocation adjustments.
   - Recommend analyzing the upcoming implementation of the Basel III Endgame rules to observe how revised capital requirements affect investment-banking operations.
