# Phase 5 Researcher Reflection Template

**Project Title**: Strategic Capital Allocation in Investment Banking: A Comparative Longitudinal Study of Major U.S. Firms, 2020–2024  
**Author**: 12th-Grade Independent Student Research Project  
**Status**: Reflection Scaffold for Student Author  

---

### Instructions for the Student Author

This template provides structured reflection prompts designed to help you synthesize what you learned from your quantitative analysis. 

As the independent researcher, you should answer these questions in your own words based on your review of the data, charts, and tables in Phase 4. None of these questions have been pre-filled.

---

### 1. What result surprised me most?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Think about whether you expected certain variables to move together (for example, whether you thought share buybacks would be more closely associated with next-year ROE, or whether you expected bank capital ratios to change more dramatically).

---

### 2. Which result makes the most economic sense to me?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Consider which findings align most intuitively with standard economic concepts, such as regular cash dividends staying steady while share buybacks adjust alongside annual earnings, or operating margins dropping when deal activity slows.

---

### 3. Which result do I find hardest to interpret?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Identify any relationship where the numbers showed unexpected signs, where Pearson and Spearman correlations diverged, or where multiple competing explanations seem equally plausible.

---

### 4. Was there a difference between what I expected and what the data showed?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Compare your original thoughts when designing Phase 1 with the actual numbers you collected from SEC Form 10-K filings.

---

### 5. What is one limitation I think matters most?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Reflect on the small sample size (five fiscal years, 12 to 16 lagged observations), the extreme market volatility between 2020 and 2024, or the fact that this study is observational and cannot prove causal mechanisms.

---

### 6. What would I collect if I repeated the study with more data?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: Consider whether you would extend the study across a 10-year or 20-year timeline, include quarterly financial reports, or examine client-level transaction databases.

---

### 7. What would I investigate next?

[STUDENT TO ANSWER PERSONALLY]

*Guidance*: What new research question does this project inspire? For example, how will investment banking capital allocation evolve under proposed Basel III Endgame regulations, or how do international investment banks (such as European firms) allocate capital compared to U.S. institutions?
